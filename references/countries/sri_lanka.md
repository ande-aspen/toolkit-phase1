# Sri Lanka - National Indicators

## P1 - Days to incorporate a business
- **Value:** 9 days (online via eROC; 5-7 working days after document submission, plus 2-3 days for name approval)
- **Source:** Department of Registrar of Companies (eROC), World Bank B-READY 2024
- **Notes:** Sri Lanka has digitized company registration through the eROC (e-Registrar of Companies) system. The entire process can be completed online. Government fees approximately LKR 13,800 (~USD 45). Companies Act No. 7 of 2007 governs the process. Foreign investors can establish 100% owned companies.

## P2 - Tax regime for startups
- **Value:** Partial incentives through BOI; no dedicated startup tax regime
- **Source:** Board of Investment of Sri Lanka, 2025 Budget, Chambers & Partners "Doing Business In 2025"
- **Notes:** Sri Lanka does not have a dedicated startup tax regime. The Board of Investment (BOI) offers tax holidays (5-10 years) and duty exemptions, but minimum investment thresholds are high (USD 250K for Section 16, USD 3M for Section 17). A flat 15% corporate tax rate applies for IT/BPO service exporters (from April 2025). Colombo Port City offers liberalized environment with additional tax incentives. The 2025 Budget recognizes entrepreneurship but remains a "mixed bag" for small startups. Standard corporate tax rate is 30%. Startups face VAT registration if turnover exceeds LKR 3M/quarter. No specific seed-stage tax exemptions or deferred social security for founders. R&D tax incentives available but primarily for larger companies.

## H5 - Internet access (% of population)
- **Value:** 53.6%
- **Source:** DataReportal Digital 2025: Sri Lanka (January 2025), Kepios analysis
- **Notes:** 12.4 million internet users out of ~23.1 million population. Internet adoption increased 7.0% (+808K users) between Jan 2024 and Jan 2025. Mobile connections: 29.3 million (127% of population), 93.3% broadband-capable (3G/4G/5G). Social media users: 8.2 million (35.4% of population). 46.4% of population remains offline.

## C5 - Social trust / entrepreneurship perception
- **Value:** Moderate; entrepreneurship not widely seen as top career choice among youth
- **Source:** GEM (limited Sri Lanka participation), British Council 2017 Social Enterprise Survey, Nature/Humanities & Social Sciences Communications 2025
- **Notes:** Sri Lanka has limited direct GEM participation. Cultural norms favor formal employment over entrepreneurship. Before the pandemic, only ~4% of enterprises were started by youth under 35. Family support is culturally important and can positively impact entrepreneurial success. Social entrepreneurship has surged across all 25 districts (British Council 2017). Government has launched entrepreneurship training programs in 2025 Budget. Fear of failure and stigma around business failure remain significant barriers. Interpersonal trust moderate by South Asian standards.

## S5 - Internet speed (default)
- **Value:** 32-63 Mbps (average broadband download; varies by provider)
- **Source:** SpeedGEO.net 2025, Ookla Speedtest via worlddata.info Q3 2025
- **Notes:** National average fixed broadband: 32.44 Mbps download, 19.70 Mbps upload. Best provider (SLT-Mobitel) averages 62.6 Mbps download. Mobile average: 27.7 Mbps (Dialog). 5G rollout in progress (2024-2025). Colombo urban areas likely above national average. Sri Lanka ranks 131st globally for fixed broadband speed.
