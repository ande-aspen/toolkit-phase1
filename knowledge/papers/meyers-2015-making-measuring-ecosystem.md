## Resumen
Practice paper (10 pp.) from U.S.SourceLink synthesizing 10+ years of experience building entrepreneurial ecosystems. Proposes a stage-dependent metrics framework, a 3-step process for constructing resource networks (map, make accessible, collaborate), and a 4-stage maturity progression (Build, Activate, Leverage, Drive) with corresponding metrics at each stage. Includes case studies from Kansas City and NetWork Kansas demonstrating program stacking and community capital mobilization.

## Insights Clave

### INS-1: Different metrics for different ecosystem stages
> After 10+ years of ecosystem building, U.S.SourceLink found that distinct metrics apply at distinct development stages. No single metric captures the dynamics of an entrepreneurial ecosystem. Key diagnostic questions span idea-to-market pipeline, startup survival, financing environment, talent pool depth, corporate engagement, startup connectivity, and exit activity.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21A
**Relevancia:** These questions map directly to the 7 ANDE domains (F, H, M, C, etc.). Supports the principle that ecosystem assessment requires multi-domain measurement, not a single index.

### INS-2: Three-step network construction process
> Step 1: Map existing assets (formal and informal, tech and non-tech). Step 2: Make resources accessible via centralized directory, calendar, and marketing campaigns working with support organizations. Step 3: Move from static directory to active collaborative network where organizations jointly identify and fill gaps.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21B
**Relevancia:** Operational framework for S1-S5 evaluation. An ecosystem with 10 disconnected organizations is less effective than one with 5 operating as an active network. For Naciente ecosystems, this 3-step process is a directly actionable recommendation.

### INS-3: Network strength metrics
> Three measurement dimensions: Access (web visits, hotline calls, directory searches), Strength (partner count, events in shared calendar, satisfaction surveys), and Reach (social media followers, traffic sources). Network organizations have a unique advantage: aggregated data on which resources are most sought, which entrepreneurs are underserved, and which programs underperform.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21C
**Relevancia:** Adds a connectivity quality layer to S1-S5 scoring beyond actor counts. Absence of shared calendar, central directory, or hotline signals weak connectivity that should reduce Support Services scores.

### INS-4: Program stacking and gap-filling -- Kansas City cases
> KCSourceLink's 240+ partner network identified urban entrepreneur challenges and created a "stacking" solution linking existing programs (Women's Business Center, SBDC, microloan) into a continuum. Result: $6M+ in new sales and 68 jobs in 18 months. Digital Sandbox KC addressed proof-of-concept funding gap with $2.1M pooled investment, producing 23 new companies and $17.7M in follow-on funding in 22 months. NetWork Kansas E-Communities grew from 6 to 48 communities and leveraged $240M+ for 450 businesses.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21D
**Relevancia:** Concrete replicable models. For Emergente ecosystems, program stacking is more efficient than creating new programs. The E-Communities model shows how small cities can mobilize local capital (relevant to F6).

### INS-5: Five-metric entrepreneurial dashboard
> Dashboard developed with UMKC and Kauffman Foundation: (1) Entrepreneurial density -- new business formation rate; (2) Deals and financing -- depth of financial support; (3) Network interconnectivity -- degree of actor connection; (4) STEM workforce -- talent pool for startups; (5) Inc. 500/5000 companies -- exceptional growth as maturity indicator.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21E
**Relevancia:** Maps to ANDE domains: Density = M1, Deals = F1-F6, Interconnectivity = S + C, STEM = H2 + H4, High-growth = M1 (growth segment). Complements ANDE rubric with operational network metrics.

### INS-6: Four-stage maturity progression
> Build (map assets, create directory) aligns with Naciente; Activate (hotline, marketing, visibility) aligns with Emergente; Leverage (identify gaps, stack programs, seek funding) aligns with En Desarrollo; Drive (dashboard, large collaborations, capital infrastructure) aligns with Autosostenible. Core finding: building ecosystem impact requires commitment to a process and patience for it to advance.

**Ubicación:** Extracto 21F
**Relevancia:** Confirms ANDE's maturity sequence with operational evidence. Recommendations in Section 7 should match the ecosystem's current stage along this Build-Activate-Leverage-Drive continuum.
